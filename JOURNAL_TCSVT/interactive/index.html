About this copy

  • All frames are model outputs generated from the benchmark's own text prompts. No third-party photographs are shown.
  • Seven systems use one common inference configuration; Steady-Forcing is its released checkpoint under its native configuration. One output per system and prompt, so nothing here ranks systems.
  • Change maps are visualizations without region masks, not metrics. Provenance and source hashes: figures/snapshots/SNAPSHOTS.json.
  • The scene specifications are unreleased benchmark material. Share this link only with collaborators.
