\subsection{Task}
The core task conditions on a fixed viewpoint and a specified natural process. The image-conditioned track uses a source image and prompt; the text-conditioned track has no common source image and requires its own partition and annotation policy.

\subsection{Scene Suites and Taxonomy}
Scenes are stratified by transport regime, the physical way material moves (for example channel flow, falling water, combustion and precipitation), and results are macro-averaged over regimes so that no regime dominates. Figure~\ref{fig:pp_scene_distribution} shows the specified scene counts, not completed generations. The four water regimes together account for 42/96 text-conditioned and 21/48 image-conditioned specifications. The text-conditioned suite specifies the same scenes at every horizon; every image-conditioned source is cut to 832$\times$480, the native resolution of the evaluated base family; any entrant whose released pipeline resizes or re-encodes is recorded as such. \pending{caption lengths, source resolution and provenance}

\subsection{Splits and Contamination Audit}
Existing source sets have informed method development and therefore are not an untouched journal confirmation cohort. Each confirmation source is checked against every development, training and earlier evaluation image by exact hash, perceptual hash and geometric verification, and sources reused from earlier evaluations are reported as a separate seen-overlap stratum. \pending{fresh rights-cleared confirmation set and its audit counts}

\subsection{Role Maps}
The planned role maps distinguish stable support, target material, moving subjects, overlays and uncertain regions, so that, for example, a moving boat and the water it floats on can be measured separately. Observable material directions are to be annotated as image-plane sectors, with uncertain and multi-direction labels. The planned protocol uses two independent annotators, reports agreement and adjudicates or marks disagreements uncertain. In the image-conditioned track, source-derived maps supply common regions across systems; the existing maps are not yet independently human-verified. The text-conditioned track has no common layout, so its planned regions require blinded per-output annotation and uncertainty reporting. \pending{annotation procedure, reviewer independence, agreement statistics}

\subsection{Horizons and Generation Protocol}
The planned primary horizons are 60, 120 and 240 seconds, with a short control. Same-rollout prefixes and separately requested durations are distinct experimental units. Each entrant runs with its released code, weights and default sampler. Native long-video inference, documented extensions and wrapped short-clip models are separate row types and are never pooled. Seeds are fixed per track. Each output's resolution, frame rate and encoder are recorded, and scoring resamples to a common analysis rate. Differences that remain are reported as part of the row type rather than removed. Final sampling, source rights, source-family splits, annotation procedures and model configurations must be frozen before the confirmation analysis. \pending{entrant table with release revision, row type, seeds and compute}
