\section{Limitations, Ethics and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:limitations}
Existing result populations have development exposure, and passing numerical parity does not establish perceptual validity. Source-relative geometry, activity and recurrence can each fail under plausible counterexamples. Real-reference bands depend on the real-footage set: they are reported with that set's size and medium coverage and are recomputed, not edited, when it grows.

Any final release must include exact input and model provenance, metric versions, failure and abstention counts, annotation uncertainty and rights-cleared material. Every source, real clip and generated video is released only under a recorded licence; an asset without one is withheld. The release follows a datasheet~\cite{gebru2021datasheets} (Appendix~\ref{app:datasheet}) and versions both the inputs and the measurement identities, so later results are never pooled with earlier ones. Human-study procedures, consent and compensation are reported with the study. \pending{maintenance plan and hosting}

Publication overlap with the original SNF-Bench~\cite{minar2026snfbench}, SceneTether~\cite{scenetether2026} and Steady-Forcing~\cite{minar2025steady} work must be disclosed. Metric and threshold selection do not use the standing of either author-affiliated method, and results are reported with and without those methods.
