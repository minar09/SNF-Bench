\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Video-generation benchmarks.} Multi-dimensional suites decompose quality into named dimensions and validate each against human preference~\cite{huang2023vbench,huang2024vbench++,Zheng2025VBench20AV,liu2024evalcrafter,liu2023fetv,sun2024t2v_compbench}. WorldScore~\cite{duan2025worldscore} adds source-anchored regional motion, and VMBench~\cite{ling2025vmbench} and Video-Bench~\cite{han2025videobench} target motion perception and judge models. VBench already reports a trade-off between temporal consistency and dynamic degree and keeps the two as separate dimensions~\cite{huang2023vbench}, and WorldScore reports static and dynamic aggregates separately, measuring geometric drift by SLAM reprojection error~\cite{duan2025worldscore}. The premise that consistency and motion must be reported separately is therefore established; what remains open in the fixed-camera, minute-scale setting is whether region-specific measures add information over these incumbents on the same videos, which Section~\ref{sec:validation} tests directly, including where incumbents already agree. These suites mostly evaluate clips of a few seconds and read each dimension in one direction.

\textbf{Dynamics, time-lapse and physics.} DEVIL~\cite{liao2024devil} grades dynamics against the prompt and DIVE~\cite{liu2025dynamici2v} rates image-to-video dynamics, both penalizing static outputs that would otherwise score well on consistency; ChronoMagic-Bench~\cite{yuan2024chronomagic} evaluates metamorphic time-lapse content, and VideoPhy~\cite{bansal2025videophy} and Physics-IQ~\cite{motamed2025physicsiq} test physical plausibility, the latter against real continuations. None of these isolates stationary support from intended motion over minutes.

\textbf{Motion and quality metrics.} Distribution metrics compare generated and real videos~\cite{Unterthiner2018TowardsAG_FVD,liu2024fvmd}. TRAJAN~\cite{allen2025trajan} scores single videos from point-track reconstruction. Quality-assessment datasets collect mean opinion scores for generated video~\cite{kou2024t2vqa,hveval2026}. We evaluate such measures as comparators and candidate statistics inside role masks; their suitability for water, smoke and fire over minutes is measured, not assumed.

\textbf{Long-horizon and fixed-camera generation.} Autoregressive and diffusion-forcing generators extend rollouts to minutes~\cite{yin2025causvid,huang2025selfforcing,liu2025rolling,yang2025longlive,helios,li2025stable}. Single-image cinemagraph methods~\cite{holynski2021animating,mahapatra2022controllable} animate a region while holding the rest still, which makes them a natural composite-type entrant.

\textbf{Entrant policy.} Every entrant is a published method evaluated as released. Steady-Forcing~\cite{minar2025steady} is entered as its public preprint, and SceneTether~\cite{scenetether2026} will be entered only once its preprint is public. Both are judged under the same rules as external methods, each as its single released configuration; no ablation or internal variant of either appears in any table. The final comparison and bibliography require verification at the journal freeze.
