\caption{\textbf{Known-answer responses of development candidates} (one model family, one seed, constructed
controls; no real footage, no human labels; separability, not validity).
(a)~\texttt{cand-replay-signature-0}, the repetition diagnostic of the authors' method
project~\cite{scenetether2026} run on SNF role maps, on 41 scenes per control: unmodified outputs and
time-reversed, shuffled, re-timed and noise-warped controls are never flagged as replay; loops and
ping-pong are; freezes are flagged as frozen.
(b)~Source-texture match of the support (normalized correlation of high-pass residuals with the source,
relative to a codec still) for unmodified outputs (three cells, 108 videos) and three composite constructions
(36 each); bars: medians. Composites separate from unmodified outputs (AUROC 0.996--1.000), but where the line
falls depends on the model family, so no threshold is set before the decoder-range ceiling is measured.
(c)~Direction referenced to the scene, not the caption, on the 22 routed scenes (6 channels, 5 falls, 11 plumes
and flames) at the frozen-spec rate: axial alignment $A$ of the material flow with the bank tangent for channels
(sign-free, $+1$ along, $-1$ across), gravity sign $G$ for falls and $-G$ for plumes and flames ($+1$ = the
physically expected way). Right: scenes scored/attempted; the rest abstain because the material moves no more than
its support. Reversal flips $G$ but leaves $A$ unchanged, as designed; every freeze abstains.
Falls are the weak route at this rate (median $G$ 0.15, 3/5 downward); sampling every frame raises it to 0.37 (4/5),
consistent with fast water outrunning the flow estimator at 8\,fps. Bars: medians per route. (d)~Sign stability of
that direction over the late window: near 1 for channels, plumes and flames on unmodified and reversed tails (falls
0.55 and 0.76), and 0.2--0.5 under ping-pong, shuffle and noise warp, which a time-averaged direction alone cannot
separate.}
\label{fig:known_answers}
