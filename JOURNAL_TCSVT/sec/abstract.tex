Long-running fixed-camera video generation requires stationary scene support and sustained, plausible motion in the intended region. Existing video quality and consistency measurements provide useful signals but do not by themselves establish whether generated motion has the right material, direction, or temporal evolution. This working manuscript specifies a measurement framework for text- and image-conditioned evaluation that separates support fidelity, material behavior, temporal continuity and human judgments. Its development draws on earlier SNF-Bench evaluations and a source-fixed image-to-video protocol. The independent journal confirmation set, experimental panel and human-alignment results remain to be collected; this draft makes no claim that the candidate measures have been validated.
