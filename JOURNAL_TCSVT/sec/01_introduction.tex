\section{Introduction}

\IEEEPARstart{I}{n} a fixed-camera video of a river, the water should move while the banks stay in place. Long generated videos can fail in either direction: the landscape may drift while the river moves, or the image may stay stable because the river has frozen. A useful evaluation must show both failures separately.

Existing benchmarks already measure important aspects of video quality, motion and consistency~\cite{huang2023vbench,huang2024vbench++,Zheng2025VBench20AV,liu2024evalcrafter,duan2025worldscore}. Our question is whether a fixed-camera evaluation adds useful information on the same videos, not whether those benchmarks are unable to see the problem. The distinction matters at long horizons, where small changes can accumulate over minutes~\cite{huang2025selfforcing,liu2025rolling,yang2025longlive,helios,li2025stable}.

SNF-Bench++ evaluates text-to-video and image-to-video generation for fixed-camera scenes with continuous natural motion. It separates the \emph{background}, which should remain stable, from the \emph{material}, such as water or smoke, that the prompt asks to move. We report background displacement and motion alongside early and late material motion. We also study checks for motion location, direction, repetition and plausibility. These checks are candidates until their controls, real-footage comparisons and human studies are complete.

The original SNF-Bench preprint~\cite{minar2026snfbench} and the source-fixed method-paper protocol are prior work. Appendix~\ref{app:prior} will distinguish their contributions from this journal study. Human and animal subjects are possible extension tracks; they are not evidence for the present nature-flow task.

This working manuscript sets out:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A scene suite} for both generation tracks, with planned 60, 120 and 240 second horizons and documented region maps (Section~\ref{sec:benchmark}).
\item \textbf{Separate measurements} of background change, material motion and temporal behavior, with explicit limits and abstentions (Section~\ref{sec:measurement}).
\item \textbf{A validation plan} using targeted controls, real footage, human judgments and comparisons on shared inputs (Section~\ref{sec:validation}).
\item \textbf{An evaluation plan} for released long-video generators (Section~\ref{sec:results}). \pending{independent panel, human validation and headline findings}
\end{itemize}
