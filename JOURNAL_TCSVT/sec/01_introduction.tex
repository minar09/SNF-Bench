\section{Introduction}

\IEEEPARstart{F}{ixed-camera} animation appears in nature observation and sustained visual content. A plausible output must preserve stable support without freezing the intended moving material, while avoiding replay, drift and scene corruption. General-purpose video benchmarks already measure important aspects of quality and consistency~\cite{huang2023vbench,huang2024vbench++,Zheng2025VBench20AV,liu2024evalcrafter,duan2025worldscore}; a task-specific benchmark must demonstrate its additional value on shared inputs rather than assume those measures are blind to fixed-camera failure.

Long-horizon generators are now evaluated at minute scale~\cite{huang2025selfforcing,liu2025rolling,yang2025longlive,helios,li2025stable}, yet two failure modes of this setting are rewarded by measures that read a statistic in one direction. Activity measures reward any motion, including incoherent or boiling motion. Source-referenced fidelity measures reward any support that stays close to the source, including a pasted still image. A fixed-camera benchmark therefore needs measures that are two-sided, region-aware and validated against real footage.

We study a versioned evaluation protocol for text-to-video and image-to-video outputs. The contribution is an independently validated measurement and evaluation resource, not a new generation method. The original SNF-Bench preprint~\cite{minar2026snfbench} and the source-fixed method-paper protocol are prior evidence; Appendix~\ref{app:prior} states what is new here. Human and animal animation are possible separately validated extension tracks, not evidence for the present nature-flow core.

The contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A fixed-camera benchmark suite} for text- and image-conditioned generation at 60, 120 and 240 seconds, with a regime taxonomy, source-derived role maps, contamination-audited splits and a datasheet (Section~\ref{sec:benchmark}).
\item \textbf{A measurement suite} that separates support fidelity, material behavior, continuity and two-sided realism, with explicit eligibility and abstention (Section~\ref{sec:measurement}).
\item \textbf{A validation protocol} in which every measure must pass constructed adversaries, real-footage bands and blinded human judgments before admission (Section~\ref{sec:validation}).
\item \textbf{An evaluation of published long-horizon generators} under one protocol, with per-regime results and findings (Sections~\ref{sec:results} and~\ref{sec:findings}). \pending{panel size and headline findings}
\end{itemize}
