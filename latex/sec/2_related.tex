\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Video generation evaluation.}
General-purpose benchmarks assess complementary aspects of visual quality, temporal consistency, motion, and semantic alignment; VBench~\cite{huang2023vbench}, for example, decomposes quality into dimensions including Background Consistency, Motion Smoothness, Temporal Flickering, and Dynamic Degree. These suites enable broad comparison across heterogeneous generators, but their measurements are primarily whole-frame or sequence-level. In fixed-camera nature-flow scenes, whole-frame aggregation cannot reveal whether measured motion originates in intended dynamic regions or in displacement of nominally static structure. SNF-Bench complements such suites by making the spatial origin of motion explicit; it replaces none of them.

\paragraph{Long-horizon autoregressive generation.}
Autoregressive and streaming generators extend synthesis beyond short bidirectional clips by producing frames or temporal chunks causally, including CausVid~\cite{yin2025causvid}, Self-Forcing~\cite{huang2025selfforcing}, Infinite-Forcing~\cite{infinite-forcing}, Rolling-Forcing~\cite{liu2025rolling}, Reward-Forcing~\cite{lu2025reward}, LongLive~\cite{yang2025longlive}, and Causal-Forcing~\cite{zhu2026causal}. These systems differ substantially in temporal block size, training objective, memory and cache policy, motion prior, and horizon-extension mechanism~\cite{yesiltepe2025infinity}. Their evaluation, however, typically relies on generic video metrics or method-specific qualitative analysis. The deployment diversity motivates SNF-Bench's two-setting audit---native capability under each released configuration, and a separately reported matched stress test---while the evaluation gap motivates its method-agnostic question: does static support remain fixed while dynamic flow persists?

\paragraph{Image dynamics and nature-flow animation.}
Work on animating still images has long modeled natural motion explicitly: controllable fluid animation separates static image structure from specified fluid motion~\cite{mahapatra2022controllable}, and Generative Image Dynamics models long-term scene motion through learned trajectory representations~\cite{Li_2024_gen_image_dynamics}. These works affirm that static support and dynamic content are distinct modeling targets---the same distinction SNF-Bench elevates to an evaluation principle for modern long-horizon generators.

\paragraph{Image-to-video faithfulness.}
I2V generation adds a reference signal: the source image specifies the initial scene. Existing I2V evaluation commonly measures source or subject consistency alongside temporal quality, but high source similarity does not determine whether motion remains confined to regions expected to move, nor whether stability was achieved by suppressing motion. SNF-I2V applies the same static--dynamic decomposition as SNF-T2V, adding source-referenced static-region fidelity as one complementary factor.
