\paragraph{Motion persistence.} Dynamic-region behavior is read at the joint point $\bigl(\mathrm{MCFF}(\mathcal{L}),\,\mathrm{FP}\bigr)$, never FP alone: sustained flow needs non-trivial late motion \emph{and} high retention. MCFF-E beside MCFF-L exposes the degenerate case, since negligible motion throughout cannot reach a high FP without a small MCFF-E.

\paragraph{Operating regimes.} Read jointly, the static-fidelity and persistence columns separate low-drift/low-motion, high-motion/high-drift and low-drift/non-trivial-motion behavior without collapsing the axes into a scalar score; the supplementary material plots this. The extremes are informative in opposite directions. The Infinite-Forcing checkpoint under the common setting~\cite{infinite-forcing} has the lowest normalized background flow: a paired bootstrap over per-prompt differences separates it from all six other checkpoints, every interval excluding zero (supplementary). It also shows the least surviving late motion. The Causal-Forcing checkpoint under the common setting~\cite{zhu2026causal} occupies the opposite corner, with substantially higher background flow.


\subsection{Whole-Frame Motion and Static-Region Flow Yield Different Interpretations}
\label{sec:results_drift}

The effect is systematic rather than anecdotal. Correlating each generic whole-frame metric with each SNF-Bench factor over checkpoint-level means (supplementary), Dynamic Degree tracks static-region flow at $\rho = \SNFddNBF$: under the common setting, checkpoints with more apparent motion also tend to have more background motion. The Causal-Forcing checkpoint ranks \SNFrankCFDD{} of \SNFnPublicTTV{} on Dynamic Degree and \SNFrankCFNBF{} on NBF; the Infinite-Forcing checkpoint mirrors it, ranking \SNFrankIFNBF{} on static fidelity and \SNFrankIFDD{} on apparent motion. Read with its magnitudes, the Causal-Forcing checkpoint's DLR of \SNFvalCFDLR{} indicates static-region motion energy almost equal to the region intended to move, while its DAR of \SNFvalCFDAR{} indicates that a rigid global model accounts for only part of that motion. What the residual contains is not identified here---it may include non-rigid instability, intended local motion, partition contamination or flow error---and these measurements do not separate them.
