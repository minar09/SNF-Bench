\subsection{Native-Capability Audit}
\label{sec:results_native}

Table~\ref{tab:t2v_audit} reports SNF-Bench measurements at the $60$\,s horizon, the tier whose sample supports method-level separation; all four horizons appear in the supplementary material.

\paragraph{Motion persistence.} Dynamic-region behavior is evaluated at the joint operating point $\bigl(\mathrm{MCFF}(\mathcal{L}),\,\mathrm{FP}\bigr)$, never FP alone: sustained flow requires non-trivial late motion \emph{and} high retention. Reporting MCFF-E beside MCFF-L makes the degenerate case visible---a system with negligible motion throughout cannot reach a high FP without also displaying a small MCFF-E.

\paragraph{Static--dynamic operating regimes.} Figure~\ref{fig:regimes_native} places each system in the static-drift--surviving-flow plane, exposing qualitatively different regimes---low-drift/low-motion, high-motion/high-drift, and low-drift/non-trivial-motion---without collapsing the axes into a scalar score. The two extremes are informative in opposite directions. Infinite-Forcing has the lowest normalised background flow, and is the only system whose interval on that factor excludes every other, while also showing the least surviving late motion; on feature-based drift its interval overlaps the next-ranked system, so it is not the least-drifting system outright. Causal-Forcing sits alone at the opposite corner, separated on background flow by a wide margin.

\subsection{Drift Leakage Changes the Interpretation of Apparent Motion}
\label{sec:results_drift}

The effect is systematic rather than anecdotal. Table~\ref{tab:disagreement} correlates each generic whole-frame metric with each SNF-Bench factor over method-level means: Dynamic Degree tracks static-region flow at $\rho = \SNFddNBF$, so the systems a generic metric reads as most dynamic are, closely, those whose backgrounds move most. Causal-Forcing ranks \SNFrankCFDD{} of \SNFnPublicTTV{} on Dynamic Degree and \SNFrankCFNBF{} on NBF; Infinite-Forcing mirrors it, ranking \SNFrankIFNBF{} on static fidelity and \SNFrankIFDD{} on apparent motion. Read with its magnitudes, Causal-Forcing's DLR of \SNFvalCFDLR{} indicates static-region motion energy almost equal to the region intended to move, while its DAR of \SNFvalCFDAR{} indicates a rigid global model accounts for only part of it---the remainder is non-rigid instability.
