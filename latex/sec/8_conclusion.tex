
\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}

SNF-Bench is intentionally narrow: long-horizon fixed-camera generation, where static support and dynamic flow can be meaningfully separated. It does not measure physical realism, and persistence cannot distinguish genuine progression from repetition. The partition is derived automatically rather than from annotation, so a system that drifts early can influence its own partition, and layered content cannot be represented by a two-way split; the transition band and the erosion--dilation study bound this without removing it. Conclusions unstable under partition perturbation are not reported as headline claims, and all measurements inherit the failure modes of the underlying estimators. Human-verified first-frame annotation is the clearest strengthening of this component. Whole-frame metrics accurately answer whether a video moves; SNF-Bench answers where that motion originates and whether it lasts.
