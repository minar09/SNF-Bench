
\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scope.}
SNF-Bench is intentionally narrow: long-horizon fixed-camera generation, where static support and dynamic flow can be meaningfully separated. It does not rank general-purpose generation across moving-camera cinematography, articulated action, narrative scene change, or interactive simulation, and its results should be read as evidence about this controlled regime.

\paragraph{Persistence is not realism.}
The benchmark measures whether motion occurs in the expected regions, remains non-trivial over time, and is uncontaminated by background displacement. It does not determine whether water, smoke, or fire obey physically correct dynamics: a sequence can be drift-free and persistent while remaining implausible, and persistence cannot distinguish genuine progression from a repeated cycle. Perceptual evaluation and physics-aware diagnostics remain complementary rather than superseded.

\paragraph{The partition and the estimators are approximate.}
The partition is derived automatically from early-window flow rather than from annotation, so a system that drifts within the opening of its rollout can influence its own partition, and layered content such as rain crossing static support cannot be represented by a two-way split. The transition band and the erosion--dilation study bound this uncertainty without removing it, and conclusions unstable under partition perturbation are not reported as headline claims. All measurements likewise inherit the failure modes of optical-flow and feature estimators: controlled validation establishes their behaviour over the benchmark's operating range, and they should be read as calibrated diagnostics rather than exact physical decompositions. Human-verified first-frame annotation is the natural strengthening of this component and the clearest direction for a subsequent version.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
We introduced SNF-Bench, an evaluation framework for long-horizon fixed-camera video generation built on one distinction: some regions of a scene must hold still, and others must not stop moving. The framework measures static fidelity, flow persistence together with absolute magnitude, and drift leakage; it validates every reported factor against corruptions of known type and severity before any system is compared; and it audits publicly released generators in the configurations their authors intended. Whole-frame metrics answer whether a video moves and whether it is consistent, and they answer that accurately. SNF-Bench answers what those aggregates cannot: where the motion originates, and whether it lasts. As horizons extend from seconds to minutes, that spatial accounting becomes the difference between a scene that flows and one that merely drifts.
