# Journal venue assessment for the standalone SNF paper

**2026-11-01 decision:** IEEE TCSVT is the selected target. The newer
[TCSVT execution protocol](TCSVT_JOURNAL_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL_2026_11_01.md)
supersedes this document’s provisional venue-selection timing. IEEE Access is
excluded by the author. Current SCIE/JCR verification remains pending.

Checked 2026-10-30 KST. Venue fit below is a recommendation, not an acceptance
prediction. Exa's account returned a credit-limit error; official publisher
and society pages were checked with the available web tool. Current ACM DL
and ScienceDirect author pages were partly inaccessible. No Q1/Q2 ranking,
SCIE status for every title, decision-time guarantee or high acceptance rate
is inferred from third-party aggregators.

| Venue | Fit and decision | Verified source / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| IEEE TCSVT | Provisional first target for a strong video measurement methodology with human validation and a reusable system | Society scope covers video processing, analysis/synthesis, learning and systems; expanded conference work may be submitted. This establishes fit, not readiness or guaranteed acceptance |
| ACM TOMM | Strong alternative for a multimedia evaluation resource, subjective analysis and reusable tooling | SIGMM describes multimedia content understanding, systems and user experience; the accessible author guide is legacy guidance. Current submission and extension rules must be rechecked |
| CVIU | Conditional option for a generalizable vision measurement/analysis contribution | Publisher-indexed scope includes image analysis, motion and vision systems; current full author guidance was inaccessible in this check |
| Pattern Recognition | Conditional option if the final work establishes a broader methodological advance | Do not assume that a larger evaluation set alone establishes scope fit; direct publisher scope/author pages were inaccessible |
| The Visual Computer | Plausible visual-computing/graphics application option | Publisher scope spans graphics, vision and AI and explicitly lists SCIE indexing. Its displayed first-decision median is not a peer-review or acceptance guarantee |
| IEEE Access | Applied measurement/resource option once technical validation is complete | Official scope includes experiments, measurement techniques and negative results; the publisher advertises 4–6 weeks submission to publication, not a guaranteed first decision or acceptance |

The IEEE Access author page permits appropriately referenced expanded
conference work, requires disclosure in the manuscript and cover letter, and
states its own similarity condition. This is not a universal rule for other
journals or a substitute for scientific distinctness. TCSVT likewise allows
expanded conference publications, which does not imply that reusing all
SceneTether results establishes a new benchmark contribution.

Choose the actual target after independent measurement results are available.
If institutional credit requires SCIE and a particular JCR quartile, verify
the exact title/ISSN in the current Clarivate record and record the JCR year
and subject category. SJR quartiles and JCR quartiles are not interchangeable.
Do not rank journals by purported acceptance probability or reuse unsourced
“high acceptance rate” advice. Review timing, fees and indexing must be
rechecked at submission.

The publication-overlap ledger must include the SNF preprint/WACV history,
SceneTether and Steady-Forcing. Current CVPR guidance explicitly addresses
overlapping journal submissions during review; the sequential plan avoids
making unverified assumptions about a concurrent related submission.
[CVPR 2027 author policy](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines).
