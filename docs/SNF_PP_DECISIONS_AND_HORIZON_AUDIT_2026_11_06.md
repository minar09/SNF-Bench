## Colour drift decision

The saved reports agree with the stated development medians and support reporting material and support separately. `cand-material-drift-0` computes Euclidean distance between **mean OpenCV 8-bit encoded Lab vectors**, not CIEDE2000 or standard ΔE76. It is a colour-stability diagnostic, not a freeze, physical-realism or compositing detector. Its historical `delta` field is retained but is confounded and should not headline. The scorer does not currently validate the control sidecar's full provenance chain; audit this before treating these values as confirmation. For frozen clips, different temporal averaging and held-frame content can change the comparison, so the observed increase is not a freeze-detection result.
