# Realism development round 1: independent decision review

2026-11-05. Review of the checked-in and uncommitted working tree, the split record, candidate implementations and their saved development reports. No videos or scores were generated for this review.

## Decision

Continue R1 as a diagnostic and R3/R4 as **development candidates**. Do not admit any realism measure or present any model as directionally correct/incorrect from this round. R2 and R5 remain open. The next useful work is robust controls, scene-specific annotation, a real-footage pilot, and blinded human evaluation. The negative findings are informative and should remain in the worklog.

* **R1:** background-change differences on composites are conditional on this generator and construction. Boundary-step equality in the FramePack comparison is a development finding, not proof of equal distributions or general indistinguishability. Edge spill's near-zero denominator makes its present numerical values unreliable. Its separately versioned replacement should abstain based on a predefined, codec-aware denominator reliability rule, and report coverage; never just increase the arbitrary epsilon.
* **R3:** reverse flips 81% on the eligible sample, not a verified accuracy on natural motion. A positive flux is agreement with a text-derived vector, not an independently verified direction label. Do not call 56–75% accuracy. The freeze result is 42/42 abstentions for mixed reasons: 36 low-flow cases, five scenes with no direction and one empty dynamic region. Falling-water and rising-level explanations are hypotheses pending footage and annotations; do not exclude an entire medium solely because it performs badly on development data. Flux near zero can mean low net direction, counterflow or estimator failure. Speed ×2 has scene-dependent failures and a 1.31 median magnitude response; neither sign nor speed invariance is established.
* **R4:** recurrence-1 separates the constructed exact and cross-faded repeated controls from the pass-throughs in this sample, but an observed development maximum of 0.941 does not set a validated threshold. Parent outputs are not independently verified loop-free. Controls repeat a 3-second segment, whereas the candidate searches lags of at least 2 seconds within a 24-second late window; evaluation must include varying durations, phase, partial masks, variable periods and periods longer than the observation window. Evaluate early/middle/late windows and their coverage as well as natural periodic scenes. Note that short periods can recur at multiples above the minimum lag. The freeze candidate has 11 non-abstentions among 41 eligible cases; activity and seam must also be calibrated under codec changes. The current seam ratio can explode when median activity approaches zero (freeze median about 11,881), independently of edge spill; apply a reliability guard under a new identity.

## Evidence and provenance caveats

1. The frozen split record correctly lists new, currently unavailable scenes for scene-held-out confirmation, but its `model_families.confirmation` also says published-panel renders on existing v2 scenes. Those are **model-family-held-out only**, not scene-held-out. Define a factorial report: seen scenes/new families, new scenes/seen families, and new scenes/new families; the last supports both generalization claims. SceneTether and H1-FM do not become family-held-out through new scenes. Do not edit the historical frozen manifest; add an amendment referencing its hash.
2. The split timestamps and saved hashes support an audit trail, but alone do not prove what people had previously viewed. Preserve exposure logs and lock independently collected confirmation data before any metric tuning or inspection.
3. Reproducing two controls byte-for-byte supports the specified software/encoding reconstruction for those cases. Matched encoding history removes a known asymmetry, but does not prove encoding differences cannot explain any measured effect: content-dependent compression and control-specific artifacts remain. Test codec/bitrate changes on a prespecified subset and check that differences are not explained by those artifacts.
4. `native_controls.adopt` uses `rec.get(key, parent_sha)` when checking the recorded parent hash. If a required key is absent, this silently substitutes the expected value. A read-only check found the required key present in all 48 records of each of the three composite RUN files; the fail-open path is therefore not triggered by missing keys in those records. Fix the adoption path to fail closed before using it for new controls. An output sidecar created by adoption is not independent evidence of the original parent relationship. Preserve the original records and audit evidence.
5. The `--reference` mode is whatever directory name is supplied first in the direction and replay scripts; provenance currently verifies hashes but not that the first mode is actually an unchanged pass-through. Require an explicit role/mode check before a new comparison. The support pilot's static input is hashed but is not checked with the same chain in the relevant path; constrain claims involving it until audited.
6. Temporal controls use fixed 16 fps encoding and a fixed 3-second base period. Verify the parent timing, processing and realized control duration/phase in the sidecars before generalizing the pipeline to other sources. The matched control results are not real-footage false-positive rates.

## People and study decisions

* Use a mixed collection strategy: a project member with a tripod-capable camera or a paid local videographer for an 8–12-scene pilot, plus rights-cleared external clips to cover missing media. A person has not been identified and must agree to the work. Lock file provenance, capture metadata, rights and scene/site separation. Several sites/sessions are needed; independent-looking clips from one recording are not independent scenes.
* Recruit two independent human annotators with relevant visual annotation experience and a third adjudicator; do not invent names. Start with the four priority direction disagreements and the empty-role scene, but also blind and randomly sample routine cases to avoid a disagreement-only annotation set. Annotate visible direction, material, ambiguity and unobservable status without showing metric scores or model identity.
* Recommend sending the prepared human-question proposal to the study owner when explicitly instructed to do so. No contact was made during this review. Maintain distinct persistence, plausible motion, direction, replay, correct material and background-coherence questions. Ask whether the existing design supports per-video ratings, source-clustered uncertainty, and sufficiently long context.

## Recommended sequence

1. Add an immutable amendment clarifying the split and audit the adopted controls' actual recorded parent keys; repair fail-open provenance handling and add tests before creating controls.
2. Define new identities and synthetic tests for low-denominator and frozen-input abstentions, including seam; retain old reports and values. Compare coverage as well as estimates across codecs.
3. Build development-only varied-loop and partial-region controls, validate transformation indices and durations, then test on a second *development* parent family. Do not use the withheld families for tuning. Document the corresponding sampling and compute budget.
4. In parallel, prepare collection and annotation protocols and recruit actual people. Obtain real fixed-camera footage before claiming false-alarm rates on natural phenomena; predefine independent site and material strata.
5. Run a blinded human pilot; use its uncertainty to plan the main study. Freeze candidates, model family exposure and thresholds before evaluating genuinely new scenes and families, then compare appropriate incumbents on matched inputs.
